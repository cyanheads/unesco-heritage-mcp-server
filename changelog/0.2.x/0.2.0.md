# 0.2.0 — 2026-10-02

## Added

- **`unesco_search_geoparks`** searches UNESCO Global Geoparks (`eg0001`) by keyword, `country`, `transnational`, designation years, or `near`, with six sorts, cursor paging, and transnational and top-country facets ([#1](https://github.com/cyanheads/unesco-heritage-mcp-server/issues/1)). A year range starting at or before 2015 adds a notice that geoparks recognized before the designation existed are dated 2015.
- **`unesco_get_geopark`** returns one geopark's full record by `ugg_id` in any case: countries, designation year, area and population as recorded, coordinates, introduction, description, the account of sustaining local communities, website, and UNESCO page; an unknown id fails as `geopark_not_found`.
- **`unesco://geopark/{ugg_id}`** resource returns the `unesco_get_geopark` record as JSON, plus a `sources` array.
- **`include_description`** on `unesco_search_sites`, `unesco_search_biosphere_reserves`, and `unesco_search_geoparks` (default `true`): `false` drops each row's `description` or `introduction`, and site results add `descriptions_omitted: true` ([#6](https://github.com/cyanheads/unesco-heritage-mcp-server/issues/6)). A cursor continues across a toggle.
- **`nearest_component`** on `unesco_search_sites` rows names the component `distance_km` was measured to, when one is nearer than the representative point.
- **Public hosted endpoint** at `https://unesco-heritage.caseyjhand.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP), listed under `remotes` in `server.json`.

## Changed

- **`unesco_list_reference`**: `countries` rows gain `geopark_count` and `datasets` lists `eg0001` with its coverage notes; both topics now load the geopark snapshot too.
- **Text cleanup** in every dataset turns `<ul>`/`<li>` list markup, raw or entity-encoded, into line breaks and decodes `&nbsp;`.
- **Server instructions** name the geopark tools, and a geopark's coordinates work as `near` for the site and reserve searches.

## Fixed

- **`unesco_search_sites` `near`** matches a site when its representative point or any component lies within `radius_km` ([#5](https://github.com/cyanheads/unesco-heritage-mcp-server/issues/5)); `distance_km` and the `distance` sort use the nearest of those points, and the zero-hit notice counts only sites with no coordinates at all.
- **`unesco_search_intangible_heritage`** adds its 2008 notice to any year range that starts at or before 2008, so a range ending before 2008 explains its empty result ([#7](https://github.com/cyanheads/unesco-heritage-mcp-server/issues/7)).
